# TierListBot - Terms of Service

_Last updated: 2026-05-08_

---

## 1. About TierListBot

TierListBot is an independent, open-source Discord bot for creating and sharing tier lists inside Discord servers. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to any external tier list platform or website.

---

## 2. Acceptable Use

By using TierListBot in your server, you agree that you will **not** use it to post, share, or rank:

- Sexually explicit or pornographic content
- Content that depicts or promotes violence, self-harm, or abuse
- Hate speech, slurs, or content targeting individuals or groups based on race, ethnicity, religion, gender, sexual orientation, disability, or national origin
- Content that harasses, doxxes, or threatens any individual
- Illegal content of any kind, including content that violates copyright or intellectual property law
- Content designed to mislead, scam, or defraud others

Server administrators are responsible for enforcing these rules within their own communities.

---

## 3. No Affiliation

TierListBot is a standalone widget. The name "tier list" is a generic term. This bot has no partnership, sponsorship, or business relationship with any external tier list service.

---

## 4. Content Responsibility

TierListBot does not moderate uploaded images or labels. You are solely responsible for the content you submit. If your server hosts content that violates these terms, access to the bot may be revoked.

---

## 5. Data & Privacy

TierListBot stores the following data to operate:

- Discord server (guild) ID and channel ID
- Discord user IDs of list creators and contributors
- Uploaded images (stored locally on the host server)
- Tier list names, item labels, and tier assignments

This data is used exclusively to render and display tier lists. It is not sold, shared, or used for advertising.

TierListBot is provided as-is, with no guarantee of uptime, data retention, or continued development. The bot may be updated, restricted, or shut down at any time.
